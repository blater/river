# Qualify riverd operational and durable recovery behavior

## Outcome

Accepted operational and power-loss recovery evidence for macOS/APFS,
Linux/ext4 and XFS, and Windows/NTFS, under the declared storage configurations.

## Scope and acceptance

Use the installed lifecycle already checked by `tic-95e8`. Exercise the complete
start/restart/stop/ps, multiple-instance, audit/archive, and credential-renewal
paths with the ADR's failure and recovery cases. Qualify the real database
WAL/control-file and launcher publication operations, including namespace
changes, against unclean shutdown and power loss. Verify committed data,
ownership, authentication, cleanup, and absence of secret leaks after recovery.
Record the platform/provider/storage assumptions and fault method. Reuse
unchanged evidence with an impact rationale; repeat affected tests when those
mechanisms or assumptions change. No per-JAR qualification manifest is required.

## Stop boundary

Evidence only: no product fixes, new harness, receipt framework, service manager,
or cross-database performance campaign. Declare cases and required test hosts
before execution. A missing host or power-loss test facility remains an explicit
validation dependency, not a reason to simulate successful qualification or
build a lab framework inside this ticket. Preserve the first unexplained failure
and create a focused defect ticket. Every required platform must pass before
this ticket closes; record and push the accepted evidence and checkpoint.
